Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you buy.

Do I need an account to buy data?

No. Guest checkout is available. An account helps you keep your orders and transaction history together.

How do I buy a bundle?

Choose your network and bundle, enter the recipient's Ghana number, then select a payment method.

How long is the bundle valid?

The prices listed on this website are for 30 days validity.

Can I send data to another person's number?

Yes. Enter the recipient's Ghana phone number during checkout.

What if I enter the wrong number?

Verify the recipient number before payment. Wrong-number, Broadband SIM or Turbonet SIM purchases should be treated as non-refundable unless your actual business policy says otherwise.

How can I track my order?

Open Track Order and enter the Order ID shown after checkout.
